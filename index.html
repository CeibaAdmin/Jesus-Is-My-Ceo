01Mission & Vision

Somebody is already running your company.

The name isn't a slogan. It's an org chart. Jesus holds the top seat over our businesses — the vision, the direction, the final call — and we operate one line beneath Him.

Most of us learned to lead by taking ownership. Set the vision, own the outcome, carry the weight. That instinct builds companies, and it also quietly makes us the highest authority in the room. This group exists to correct the chart. You still run the company. You just stop pretending you own it.

Practically, that means we bring real decisions to a table of people who will ask what God is saying before they ask what the market is saying — and who will follow up next month to see whether you actually listened.

  • Vision

    We seek direction from the One who sees the whole market — and the whole story.

  • Direction

    Strategy submitted in prayer before it's submitted to a spreadsheet.

  • Accountability

    Peers who know your numbers, your temptations, and your commitments.

  • Reporting

    Every owner reports to someone. We report to Christ, out loud, together.

02Our Guiding Principle

Stewardship

Everything is borrowed. God is the ultimate owner of everything we have, had, and will ever have.

If the business isn't ours, the pressure changes shape. Decisions stop being about protecting what we built and start being about managing well what we were handed — which frees us to be generous when it's costly, patient when it's slow, and honest when a shortcut would be easier to explain.

It reaches the finances first: how we price, how we pay people, how much we keep and how much we release. Then it reaches growth, because a steward asks whether an opportunity is his to take before he asks whether he can win it. And it reaches relationships — clients, employees, competitors, and the people at this table are all entrusted to us, not owed to us.

07Questions

Before you reach out.

Who is this group for?

Christian business owners and entrepreneurs who want their faith to actually govern how they operate — not sit beside it. If you carry decisions nobody else in your life fully understands, and you want peers who will pray about them and then hold you to what you said, you're the person we built this for.

Do I need to be a certain type of business owner to join?

No. Solo operators, partners, and owners with teams are all welcome, and we intentionally keep industries varied — a directory full of the same trade helps nobody. What matters is that you own or lead the business and can speak to its decisions. [ANY ADDITIONAL CRITERIA]

How do I join?

There's no form here on purpose. Membership runs through Church by the Glades, and our leaders add new members personally so the group stays a room of people who actually know each other.

Two ways to start: connect through Church by the Glades and ask about the Jesus Is My CEO lifegroup, or reach out to Kelly or Alex directly using the contact details below. Expect a real conversation before you ever attend — that's the point.

Is there a cost?

[COST/DUES POLICY] If anything changes, you'll hear it from a leader before it affects you, never as a surprise.

How does the referral and directory piece work?

Members are listed in the directory with their business, category, and a short description. When a need comes up — in a meeting or in the group chat — we look to members first and make a direct introduction.

There are no required referral counts, no lead fees, and no obligation to hire another member. A referral here is a personal endorsement, so we'd rather you pass along five you'd stake your name on than fifty you wouldn't. [ANY DIRECTORY GUIDELINES]

What if I'm not sure I belong in a group like this?

Come and see. Most people arrive unsure — either they think their business is too small to be worth discussing or too complicated to bring into a faith conversation. Neither has been true yet. Reach out and we'll talk about whether this is the right table for you.
